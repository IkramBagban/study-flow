# Cognitive Science Foundations
*The "why" behind every feature in StudyFlow.*

---

## 1. The Core Differentiator: "Brain-Aligned"
Most platforms optimize for **content delivery** (videos, text). We optimize for **encoding** (how the brain actually writes memory).

## 2. The 3 Primary Laws

### Law 1: The schema Pre-Requisite
> "You cannot learn a leaf if you don't know the branch."
-   **Science:** The brain organizes info in **Schemas** (mental maps). If a new fact doesn't fit into an existing map, it is discarded as noise.
-   **In App:** We never show a topic without showing its parent in the **Domain Map**.
-   **Mechanism:** **0-Idea State Detection.** If a user lacks the "Hook," we seed it with *Name* and *Purpose* before deep teaching.

### Law 2: Active Recall > Recognition
> "If it feels easy, you aren't learning."
-   **Science:** **Recognition** (seeing and nodding) uses short-term visual cache. **Recall** (retrieving without cues) builds long-term neural pathways.
-   **In App:**
    -   No "Next" button. Only "Answer" buttons.
    -   Predictions *before* Explanations.

### Law 3: Desirable Difficulty
> "The Sweet Spot between Boredom and Anxiety."
-   **Science:** **Flow State.** Learning happens when the challenge slightly exceeds current skill.
-   **In App:**
    -   Too easy? Switch from MCQ to Text Input.
    -   Too hard? Provide a hint (Scaffolding).

---

## 3. The Forgetting Curve & Spacing
-   **Problem:** Humans forget 70% of new info within 24 hours.
-   **Solution:** **Interleaved Spacing.**
    -   We do not have a "Review Day."
    -   We inject old questions into *current* study sessions.
    -   *Example:* While learning Chapter 4, you get a "warm-up" question from Chapter 1.
